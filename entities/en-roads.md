# En-ROADS

A climate policy simulator developed by [[climate-interactive|Climate Interactive]] and the MIT Sloan Sustainability Initiative. Built on [[concepts/system-dynamics|system dynamics]] modeling, En-ROADS lets users explore how policies affecting energy supply, efficiency, electrification, land use, and carbon pricing interact to determine global temperature outcomes through 2100.

> [!inference] En-ROADS is an example of a [[concepts/management-flight-simulators|management flight simulator]] applied to public policy. Like the project management simulator, it lets users test strategies in a risk-free environment — but its notable feature is that users can change the model's assumptions themselves when they do not trust the defaults.
