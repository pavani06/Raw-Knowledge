# Agentic Commerce

The thesis that AI agents conducting commercial transactions will become a larger economic
force than traditional e-commerce. "Agentic commerce will be bigger than e-commerce" — Zack
Reno Wedeen, Head of Product at [[entities/sierra|Sierra]].

This is not just automated checkout: it encompasses agents browsing for products, managing
subscriptions, canceling unused services, making dinner reservations, and handling the full
customer lifecycle — all without a human clicking through a website. The same way you ask
[[entities/codex|Codex]] or [[entities/claude-code|Claude Code]] to do things today, you will
soon ask them to manage commercial interactions.

## Key Insights

- **Personal agents as the primary interface** — most agentic commerce will flow through
  personal agents (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex) interacting with brand agents on the other side
  ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **Brands need an agent presence** — just as websites replaced storefronts, brand agents
  will be the digital presence for the agentic economy; customers (both human and agent)
  will discover and transact through them ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **Attention economics shift** — human eyeballs are currently worth more than agent tokens,
  but this may change as agent-mediated transactions accumulate in training data
  ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **Outcome-based pricing aligns incentives** — [[entities/sierra|Sierra]]'s agents earn
  commissions on sales; see [[concepts/outcome-based-pricing]] for the full model
  ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).

> [!inference] Agentic commerce is the endgame of the [[concepts/agentic-ai]] thesis applied
> to the economy: if agents can act autonomously, commerce is a natural domain. The
> infrastructure shift from "build a website" to "build an agent" mirrors the mobile-app
> transition of 2008-2012.
