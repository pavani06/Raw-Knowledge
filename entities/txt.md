# .txt

A 15-person AI company specializing in **structured outputs** (three years in the niche),
led by CEO Rémi Louf. After the late-2025 "step function" in agent capability (which he
attributes to the Opus 4.6 era), Louf took two weeks off to build a personal agent system
from scratch — which became the [[concepts/agent-kernel|agent kernel]], the company's big
dogfooding project ([[sources/2026-09-02-agent-frameworks-considered-harmful-remi-louf-txt]]).

The kernel went from scratch to internal production in about a week of part-time building,
and a month after deployment ran **20 agents** inside the company, contributed by
non-technical staff. The stack runs on open-source models (no third-party APIs; a local
model on the laptop suffices for the workload). The kernel is not a product and the code
is offered to be stolen ([[sources/2026-09-02-agent-frameworks-considered-harmful-remi-louf-txt]]).
