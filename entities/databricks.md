# Databricks

Data and AI company built on open-source technologies ([[entities/apache-spark|Apache Spark]],
[[entities/mlflow|MLflow]], [[entities/delta-lake|Delta Lake]]). Provides a unified data
and AI platform across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. The platform layers: cloud storage
→ Delta Lake (database-like properties) → Unity Catalog (governance) → AI/BI applications.

Sandipan Bhaumik, a technical lead for data and AI at Databricks, presented the 5-pillar
production AI framework based on customer engagements. Databricks' AI offerings include
[[entities/mosaic-ai|Mosaic AI]] (LLM building/tuning/deployment) and [[entities/agent-bricks|Agent Bricks]]
(production-grade AI agent platform).
