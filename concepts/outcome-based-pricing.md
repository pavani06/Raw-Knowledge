# Outcome-Based Pricing

A business model where an AI agent platform charges per **resolved outcome** rather than
per seat, per usage, or per token. The customer pays only when the agent delivers measurable
value — a resolved support issue, a completed sale, a converted membership.

"If you don't understand the value of outcome-based pricing, your outcomes are probably not
that valuable." — Zack Reno Wedeen, [[entities/sierra|Sierra]].

## Key Insights

- **Incentive alignment** — outcome pricing aligns the platform's incentives with the
  customer's: both want more resolutions, more sales, better experiences. It "cuts through
  all of the prioritization and decision-making that often cloud enterprise partnerships"
  ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **Value determines price** — high-value outcomes (selling a car, converting a membership)
  command premium pricing; commoditized outcomes (FAQ lookups, balance checks) trend toward
  usage-based or seat-based models ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **Customer-specific calibration** — pricing varies by customer and use case because the
  value of an outcome differs: troubleshooting a complex device over 20 turns vs. resetting
  a TV signal ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **Don't over-engineer** — Sierra deliberately avoids "pedantic engineering of specific
  outcomes" because the alignment benefit dwarfs the precision cost; fairness and simplicity
  matter more than perfect value attribution ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **Becoming the norm** — outcome-based pricing is expected to become the standard for
  differentiated, high-value agent activities; usage-based and seat-based will remain for
  commoditized AI ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).

> [!inference] Outcome-based pricing is the business-model counterpart of
> [[concepts/eval-driven-development]]: the eval defines success; the pricing model ties
> revenue to that same success metric. When evals and pricing align on the same outcome,
> the feedback loop between customer value and platform improvement closes.
