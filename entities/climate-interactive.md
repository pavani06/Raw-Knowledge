# Climate Interactive

A non-profit organization that co-developed the [[en-roads|En-ROADS]] climate policy simulator with the MIT Sloan Sustainability Initiative. En-ROADS allows users to explore the impact of climate policies — energy supply, efficiency, land use, carbon pricing — on global temperature through 2100.

## Mentioned In

- [[sources/2026-06-25-systems-thinking-for-leaders-designing-solutions-that-work|Systems Thinking for Leaders]] — John Sterman describes En-ROADS as a [[concepts/management-flight-simulators|management flight simulator]] for climate policy where users can change assumptions based on the best peer-reviewed evidence and see how policies affect climate harm reduction
