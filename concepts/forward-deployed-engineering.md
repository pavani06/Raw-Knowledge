# Forward-Deployed Engineering

Sierra's organizational pattern of embedding builders with customer teams: engineers and PMs work "specifically with customers in a forward deployed role," building agents alongside the domain experts who know the ideal customer experience. Sierra is "pretty famously known for your forward deployed engineering or agent builder approach" ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).

## Key Insights

- **The role** — Zack Reno Wedeen "joined Sierra as an agent PM working on building agents specifically with customers in a forward deployed role," starting with SiriusXM's voice channel, where volume "over voice" exceeded chat ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **Individual customer care as a builder's leverage** — "the process of caring about each customer individually, saying one customer is upset, I'm going to call them right now and find out why and see how I can help... felt very empowering as a builder" in contrast to building for a billion users where "it didn't feel like you could listen to each user." Customer outcomes compound into careers: "we have customers of Sierra that have gotten promoted in their organizations. They're building careers because of the agents that they built on Sierra" ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **A rare product shape: consumer-grade end user, enterprise trust process** — "the end user of a Sierra agent is still a consumer in the vast majority of cases... it's pretty rare to have a product that needs to be consumer grade... but where you have kind of the enterprise sales process of building trust, of solving problems, of discovering value." The people who thrive bring "the customer obsession and the craftsmanship" ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **Coding agents raise the bar for human judgment** — "with the rise of coding agents, certain things are more important than they used to be. Deep customer intuition, GPT-5.5 doesn't really have that. Agency, the ability to say, 'Why can't I do this?'" The F1 analogy: "a faster car needs more pit stops" — code ships faster, so product judgment and customer intuition are needed *more* often, not less; splitting judgment and engineering across two roles demands "even tighter collaboration" ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **The hybrid persona wins** — "people that have done both roles are often successful in the company" (Sierra's head of engineering was previously a product manager). Working hiring rubric: "customer intuition, agency, product judgment, technical depth, communication, intensity," plus leadership ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).
- **Interviewing for agency** — the AI-native interview has candidates "building a product end-to-end over the course of a few hours and then reviewing it with the team," revealing "what people think is off-limits or what's their job and what's not their job" — whether they see "that a lot is in their control" ([[sources/2026-06-25-the-best-ai-agents-are-simpler-than-you-think]]).

> [!inference] The "faster car needs more pit stops" insight is the staffing counterpart of [[concepts/harness-engineering]]: as execution accelerates, steering discipline (product judgment, customer intuition) becomes the scarcer resource. Forward-deployed engineering also pairs with [[concepts/no-code-agent-building]] — the platform sands down barriers so customer ops experts self-serve, while forward-deployed builders handle the deep, code-adjacent work (CI/CD, complex tools) and embody the multi-disciplinary profile the role demands.
